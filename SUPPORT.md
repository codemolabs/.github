# Getting Support at COSL

Welcome! If you need assistance with Codemo Open Source Labs (COSL) projects, labs, or documentation, here is how you can find help.

---

## 1. Join Our Community on Discord

The fastest way to ask questions, meet fellow learners, discuss lab exercises, and get interactive assistance is on our Discord server:

**[Join the COSL Discord Server](https://discord.gg/wD9d5Er7a)**

Channels available:
- `#general`: Say hello and discuss tech and open source.
- `#labs-help`: Ask questions about hands-on labs and getting started.
- `#project-ideas`: Propose features or collaborate on new labs.
- `#code-reviews`: Share your work and ask for friendly feedback.

---

## 2. GitHub Discussions & Issues

- **Project-Specific Questions:** If your question is specific to a particular repository (for example, setup instructions, bugs, or feature ideas), check that repository's **Discussions** or **Issues** tab.
- **Reporting Bugs:** If you found a bug or error in a project or documentation, please submit a bug report via the repository's issue tracker.
- **Search Before Asking:** Please search existing open and closed issues/discussions before opening a new thread to avoid duplicate conversations.

---

## 3. General & Formal Inquiries

For partnership questions, community moderation appeals, licensing clarifications, or general organization questions, you can reach out directly via email:

**[cosl@codemoteams.org](mailto:cosl@codemoteams.org)**

---

## 4. Security Inquiries

If you need to report a suspected vulnerability or security concern, please consult our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and report privately to **[cosl@codemoteams.org](mailto:cosl@codemoteams.org)** rather than opening a public issue.
