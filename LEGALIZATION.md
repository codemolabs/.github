# COSL Legal & Usage Notes

This document provides general information about contributing to and using COSL repositories. It is not legal advice and does not replace the terms, license, or policies that apply to a specific project or jurisdiction.

## Project-specific licenses govern

Each repository may have its own license and notices. Read them before copying, modifying, distributing, or incorporating code, documentation, datasets, media, or other materials. Do not assume that all COSL repositories use the same license. If a repository has no clear license, do not assume that reuse is permitted; ask the maintainers.

## Contributions and rights

Only submit material you created or are authorized to contribute. By opening a pull request, you confirm that you have the necessary rights to submit the proposed material under the project’s stated license and contribution terms, if any. A contribution does not automatically change the license of existing project material. Do not include third-party code, images, datasets, or text unless the applicable terms allow it and required attribution is included. Holding a leadership role, organizational credential, or professional certification does not alter intellectual property ownership of contributions or grant proprietary ownership over project materials beyond the applicable project license.

## Trademarks and branding

COSL names, logos, and project marks identify the organization and its projects. Their presence in a repository does not grant permission to imply endorsement, affiliation, or sponsorship. Ask the relevant maintainers before using branding in a way that could confuse people about official status.

## Privacy and public repositories

Public issues, pull requests, commits, and discussions may be visible and indexed. Do not post passwords, authentication tokens, private keys, personal data, confidential information, or private security details. Remove sensitive data from examples and logs. If you believe information has been exposed, contact the repository maintainers promptly; deleting a public post may not remove copies already made elsewhere.

## Security and educational material

COSL learning materials are for lawful, authorized education and testing. Use security exercises only on systems you own or have explicit permission to test, and follow the applicable scope and rules. Do not use COSL materials to access systems without authorization, disrupt services, steal data, or harm others. Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the relevant maintainers rather than publishing exploit details prematurely.

## No warranty; use at your own risk

Unless a repository license or applicable law says otherwise, materials are provided by their respective contributors “as is,” without promises that they are error-free, safe for a particular purpose, or suitable for production. Review and test code before relying on it. Contributors and maintainers may not provide support or updates. Repository metadata, tags, and custom properties (including compliance tracking properties) serve internal administrative purposes only and do not constitute audit certifications, legal guarantees, or formal compliance warranties.

## External links and third-party services

Links to external websites or services are provided for convenience. COSL does not control their content, privacy practices, availability, or terms. Review the provider's own policies before using them.

## Questions and corrections

For repository-specific licensing or permissions questions, contact the maintainers listed in that repository. For general COSL questions, write to [cosl@codemoteams.org](mailto:cosl@codemoteams.org). If a formal legal interpretation is needed, consult a qualified legal professional in the relevant jurisdiction.
