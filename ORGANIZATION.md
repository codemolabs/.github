# COSL Organization Structure

**Organization:** Codemo Open Source Labs (COSL)  
**Parent Ecosystem:** Codemo Teams  
**Structure:** Initial Operating Structure  
**Review:** As organizational needs and contributor capacity evolve  

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the initial organizational hierarchy, essential positions, responsibilities, and authority boundaries of Codemo Open Source Labs (COSL).

COSL maintains a small and practical organizational structure. Additional leadership positions and departments may be introduced only when operational requirements and contributor capacity justify them.

COSL operates within the Codemo Teams ecosystem and follows applicable parent-level governance and organizational policies.

---

## 2. Organizational Hierarchy

```
Codemo Teams
│
└── COSL Director
    │
    ├── Deputy Director
    │
    ├── Technical Lead
    ├── Open Source & Projects Lead
    ├── Community Lead
    └── Learning & Mentorship Lead

Operational Roles:
    ├── Project Maintainers
    ├── Mentors
    └── Contributors
```

The Director holds overall COSL organizational responsibility. The Deputy Director supports the Director and coordinates responsibilities delegated to them.

Functional Leads report to the Director or to the Deputy Director where authority has been delegated.

---

## 3. Leadership Positions

### 3.1 COSL Director
The COSL Director is responsible for the overall leadership and direction of COSL.

**Responsibilities:**
- Establish organizational priorities and objectives.
- Lead COSL operations and organizational development.
- Approve major COSL initiatives and organizational decisions.
- Appoint or authorize leadership responsibilities under applicable governance.
- Oversee functional leads and organizational accountability.
- Represent COSL within the Codemo Teams ecosystem and external collaborations.

### 3.2 Deputy Director
The Deputy Director assists the Director and supports the continuity of COSL operations.

**Responsibilities:**
- Coordinate assigned organizational activities.
- Support functional leads and operational execution.
- Assist with planning and organizational decisions.
- Handle responsibilities explicitly delegated by the Director.
- Act on behalf of the Director within the limits of delegated authority.

The Deputy Director does not automatically receive unrestricted authority beyond the assigned responsibilities.

### 3.3 Technical Lead
The Technical Lead oversees COSL's technical direction and engineering quality.

**Responsibilities:**
- Guide technical architecture and engineering standards.
- Support secure software development practices.
- Coordinate technical work across projects where needed.
- Help evaluate technical feasibility and implementation quality.
- Support engineering reviews, testing and infrastructure decisions.
- Encourage maintainable, reliable and privacy-conscious software.

Specialist responsibilities such as cybersecurity, DevOps, AI engineering and quality assurance may initially operate under this position or within relevant project teams.

### 3.4 Open Source & Projects Lead
The Open Source & Projects Lead coordinates COSL projects, laboratories and open-source collaboration.

**Responsibilities:**
- Coordinate official COSL projects and laboratories.
- Support project planning and contributor coordination.
- Help establish project goals and delivery expectations.
- Coordinate project maintainers.
- Support contributions to established upstream open-source projects.
- Develop relationships with external open-source organizations and collaborators.

### 3.5 Community Lead
The Community Lead coordinates COSL community participation and member engagement.

**Responsibilities:**
- Support Discord community operations.
- Coordinate onboarding and community participation.
- Help maintain a constructive and productive environment.
- Coordinate community activities and announcements.
- Work with the Learning & Mentorship Lead on learner engagement.
- Escalate conduct or operational concerns through the appropriate process.

### 3.6 Learning & Mentorship Lead
The Learning & Mentorship Lead coordinates COSL learning activities and contributor development.

**Responsibilities:**
- Coordinate learning programs and educational paths.
- Support practical labs, exercises and workshops.
- Organize mentor participation.
- Help learners progress toward professional development.
- Encourage practical learning through project contributions.
- Coordinate learning-related achievements and evaluations.

---

## 4. Operational Roles

Operational roles support actual projects and activities. They are not separate executive positions.

### Project Maintainer
A Project Maintainer is assigned responsibility for an individual repository, project or laboratory.

**Responsibilities:**
- Review and manage project contributions.
- Maintain project quality and technical consistency.
- Coordinate project tasks and issues.
- Support contributors working on the assigned project.
- Manage repository-level responsibilities within granted permissions.

Project Maintainers do not automatically receive organization-wide authority.

### Mentor
A Mentor supports learners and contributors through guidance, technical explanations and feedback.

**Responsibilities:**
- Assist with learning paths, laboratories, code reviews and practical development.
- Guide contributors toward constructive problem solving.

Mentorship does not automatically grant repository administration or organizational decision-making authority.

### Contributor
A Contributor participates in COSL projects, laboratories and open-source activities.

**Scope:**
- Contributions may include software development, cybersecurity research, testing, documentation, design, infrastructure and other approved work.
- Contributors operate within the relevant project guidelines and granted permissions.

---

## 5. Authority and Reporting

| Position | Authority Scope |
|:---|:---|
| **Codemo Teams** | Parent ecosystem governance and applicable parent-level decisions |
| **COSL Director** | Overall COSL organizational authority, subject to parent governance |
| **Deputy Director** | Responsibilities delegated by the Director |
| **Functional Leads** | Decisions within their assigned functional responsibilities |
| **Project Maintainers** | Assigned project and repository responsibilities |
| **Mentors** | Learning guidance and mentorship |
| **Contributors** | Assigned contribution activities |

Organizational titles do not automatically grant GitHub organization-owner privileges, repository administration, financial authority or permission to make legal commitments.

Technical permissions and administrative access must be assigned separately according to actual responsibilities and the principle of least privilege.

---

## 6. Role Appointments and Growth

- Leadership positions must be formally assigned by the appropriate COSL authority.
- Responsibilities and delegated authority must be clear.
- One person may temporarily hold more than one operational responsibility when organizational capacity is limited.
- Separate specialist positions should be introduced only when there is a genuine operational need.
- Leadership assignments should be reviewed when responsibilities or organizational requirements change.
- No person receives organizational authority merely by reaching a membership achievement level or completing a learning pathway.

---

## 7. Membership and Achievement Distinction

COSL organizational positions are separate from Codemo Teams membership types and achievement levels.

Free / Introductory Member, Student Member and Professional Member are membership classifications managed by the Codemo Teams membership system.

Learner progression, developer achievements, reputation points, badges and membership-extension milestones recognize participation or accomplishment. They do not automatically grant COSL leadership positions, project-maintainer status or GitHub permissions.

---

## 8. Future Organizational Expansion

COSL may introduce additional specialist roles or departments when:
1. The workload requires dedicated responsibility.
2. There are enough qualified contributors to support the position.
3. The responsibilities cannot reasonably remain within the existing structure.
4. The relevant COSL authority approves the position.

Potential future specializations include security, research, DevOps, quality assurance, documentation and operations. These are possible future additions, not currently required leadership positions.
