# COSL Community Rules

COSL is for learning, building, and contributing together. These rules apply to COSL spaces and project interactions, including issues, pull requests, discussions, chat spaces operated by COSL, events, and learning activities.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect, patience, and good faith. Critique ideas and work, not a person's worth or identity.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and perspectives.
- Communicate constructively. Disagreement is acceptable; harassment, intimidation, insults, and persistent disruption are not.
- Give helpful context, acknowledge others' work, and respect project maintainers' time and decisions.
- Keep feedback relevant, actionable, and appropriate to the space.

## Not acceptable

COSL does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexualized harassment, doxxing, hate speech, targeted humiliation, or repeated unwanted contact. Do not post obscene or violent content in learning spaces, impersonate others, spam, or use community channels to promote scams or unrelated commercial activity.

## Safety, security, and privacy

- Never publish passwords, tokens, private keys, personal contact details, or another person's private information.
- Do not upload malware, exploit code, or instructions intended to harm people or systems. Security learning must be authorized, scoped, and conducted in safe environments.
- Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the relevant maintainers; do not expose exploitable details in a public issue before coordinated handling.
- Do not share content, images, or data unless you have permission and a lawful basis to do so.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or remove access when needed to protect the community or project. Responses may include a reminder, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal, depending on severity and context. Maintainers may act immediately for credible threats, serious abuse, exposed secrets, or urgent security risks.

Moderation decisions should be applied fairly and proportionately. If you believe a decision was mistaken, contact the maintainers respectfully at [cosl@codemoteams.org](mailto:cosl@codemoteams.org) with relevant context. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern or participates in moderation.

## Reporting concerns

For conduct concerns, contact [cosl@codemoteams.org](mailto:cosl@codemoteams.org) and include links or details needed to understand the situation. Avoid forwarding sensitive personal data. For immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services.

## Scope and updates

Project-specific rules may add detail but should not weaken these baseline expectations. COSL may update this document as the community evolves; check the repository's current version when in doubt. For policies regarding learning achievements, certifications, and leadership qualifications, refer to [CREDENTIALS.md](CREDENTIALS.md).
